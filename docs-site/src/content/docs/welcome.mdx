A doctor is a single JS file that scans your repo for mistakes and reports
the findings. It is deterministic: same code in, same findings out. No
model, no tokens, no hallucinated problems.

You run it after your agent finishes work. Findings come back with the
file, the line, and the reason. You (or your agent) review them, fix what
is real, and record decisions on the rest. Decisions are remembered: an
accepted finding never shows up again, even after the line moves.

## The loop

1. Your AI agent makes a mistake. Then makes it again. And again.
2. You describe the mistake and write a doctor for it — or your agent
   writes the doctor while you watch.
3. Every scan from then on catches it. Re-checking costs nothing.

## What ships in the box

Five doctors cover common agent-made mistakes:

- **async** — fetch hygiene, dropped promises, timer cleanup
- **convex** — indexed reads, bounded collects, query discipline
- **slop** — duplicated helpers, dead exports, hostname-sniffed
  environments, careless text matching
- **openrouter** — stream errors surfaced, cancellations that stop billing
- **effect-v4** — the mechanical rules of the kitlangton Effect skill,
  enforced

The real product is the doctors you write yourself. Your codebase has its
own patterns. A doctor is a single JS file. Describe the mistakes to your
agent, point it at the repo, ship it to your team.
